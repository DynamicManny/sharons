How a fitting works

From first message to final fit.

  1. 1

    Send a message

    Tell us what you need and when you need it. We reply on WhatsApp.

  2. 2

    Come in to be measured

    We measure you, look at the garment or talk through the design, and agree what needs doing.

  3. 3

    We sew

    Your dress is made, or your garment is altered or repaired.

  4. 4

    Final fitting

    Try it on, we adjust anything that needs it, and you take it home.
